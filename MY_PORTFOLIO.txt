5.## Intelli-Credit — AI-Powered Corporate Credit Appraisal Engine
link:https://github.com/sushanth005/Intelli-Credit-AI-Powered-Corporate-Credit-Appraisal-Engine
Description:	
Intelli-Credit is an AI-driven corporate credit risk assessment system designed to automate and enhance business loan evaluation. Built on a **FastAPI-based backend and React frontend**, it integrates multi-format financial document parsing, structured financial data extraction, GST–bank transaction reconciliation, and web-based due diligence. A weighted **Five-Cs credit scoring engine** combines quantitative financial indicators with qualitative risk intelligence to generate explainable lending decisions, risk-based loan recommendations, and automated Credit Appraisal Memos (CAM).

**Key Features**
- **Intelligent Document Processing:** Parses financial statements, annual reports, and legal documents using PDF extraction pipelines to identify key financial indicators such as EBITDA, PAT, DSCR, ICR, and debt-to-equity ratio.
- **Financial Anomaly Detection:** Performs GST and bank statement reconciliation to identify discrepancies, potential circular trading patterns, and financial irregularities.
- **AI-Powered Due Diligence:** Uses external web search and LLM-based synthesis to analyze company profiles, promoter backgrounds, litigation records, and regulatory developments.
- **Weighted Five-Cs Risk Engine:** Implements a configurable scoring model across Character, Capacity, Capital, Collateral, and Conditions to evaluate corporate creditworthiness.
- **Automated Credit Decisioning:** Generates risk-based approval recommendations, suggested loan amounts, interest rates, and risk premiums.
- **Credit Appraisal Report Generation:** Dynamically generates downloadable PDF Credit Appraisal Memos containing financial analysis, risk assessments, and lending recommendations.

**AI Integration:** Google Gemini 1.5 Flash for financial data interpretation, research synthesis, and credit decision support; Serper.dev for real-time external information retrieval.

**Tech Stack:** Python, FastAPI, React.js, Vite, Google Gemini API, Serper.dev, pdfplumber, PyPDF2, ReportLab, Recharts, Axios.

**Use Cases:** Corporate credit risk modeling, automated loan underwriting, financial statement analysis, regulatory due diligence, and lending decision support.

- Cross-platform volunteer app with GPS reporting and offline synchronization.
